Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

Welcome to the Wadgaonkar Eye Hospital Patient Application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how Wadgaonkar Eye Hospital ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal and sensitive information when you use our App and services.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of India and the guidelines set forth by the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our medical scheduling and consultation services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, age, gender, and contact details (mobile number, email address) provided during registration.
  • Authentication Data: We use Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In to securely verify your identity.
  • Health Information: Information regarding your appointments, selected services (e.g., eye checkup), and past booking history to determine discount eligibility (e.g., 30-Day Follow-Up).
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers necessary for crash reporting and sending appointment reminder push notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used for the following purposes:

  • To schedule, manage, and track your OPD appointments.
  • To process pre-paid online payments securely via the PhonePe Payment Gateway.
  • To send you automated reminders (via push notifications) 15 minutes prior to your scheduled slot.
  • To maintain a digital medical record of your visits.

3. Data Security & Deletion

We implement robust security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure token-based API authentication, to protect your data. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

All payments made for OPD consultations (such as eye checkups) are final. We do not offer monetary refunds for cancelled appointments under any circumstances.

We do not sell physical products; we provide time-slotted medical services. When you book a slot, that time is exclusively reserved for you. Therefore, cancellations do not qualify for a refund to your bank account, UPI ID, or credit card.

2. Rescheduling (No Wasted Money)

While we do not offer refunds, your money is never wasted.

If you are unable to attend your scheduled appointment, you have the flexibility to reschedule your visit to any available future date and time slot for the same doctor and service. The pre-paid amount is fully carried over to your new rescheduled slot at no additional cost.
