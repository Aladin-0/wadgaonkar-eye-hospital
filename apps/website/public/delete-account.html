Delete Your Account

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

You can permanently delete your Wadgaonkar Eye Hospital Patient App account and personal data at any time.

Option 1: Inside the App (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account at the bottom and confirm.

Option 2: By Email

If you can no longer use the app, email wadgaonkareyeehospital@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". You can also call 02402324219. We process verified requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, mobile number, age, gender and other details you entered.
  • Your sign-in identity (Google or Apple) linked to this app, and your push notification token.

What is kept

  • Appointment and payment records are kept in anonymised form (your name, mobile number and identifiers removed) for the period required by Indian medical and tax record-keeping rules.
  • Medical records created during in-person treatment at the hospital are retained as required by medical regulations.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
