Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

Last Updated: November 3, 2026

At Wadgaonkar Eye Hospital, we value both your time and the time of our specialist consultants. To ensure smooth operations and availability for all patients, we enforce the following Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy for all OPD appointments booked through the App.

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

All payments made for OPD consultations (such as eye checkups) are final. We do not offer monetary refunds for cancelled appointments under any circumstances.

We do not sell physical products; we provide time-slotted medical services. When you book a slot, that time is exclusively reserved for you. Therefore, cancellations do not qualify for a refund to your bank account, UPI ID, or credit card.

2. Rescheduling (No Wasted Money)

While we do not offer refunds, your money is never wasted.

If you are unable to attend your scheduled appointment, you have the flexibility to reschedule your visit.

  • You can reschedule your appointment to any available future date and time slot for the same doctor and service.
  • The pre-paid amount is fully carried over to your new rescheduled slot at no additional cost.
  • To reschedule, simply navigate to the "My Appointments" section in the App and select a new available time slot.

3. Hospital-Initiated Cancellations

In the rare event that the hospital or the doctor has to cancel your appointment due to an unforeseen emergency or unavailability:

  • You will be notified immediately via the App.
  • You will be granted the option to reschedule your appointment to the next earliest available slot of your convenience, ensuring your pre-paid fee is fully utilized.
